1. Scan your mailbox

Drop a mailbox file here, or click to choose Supports .mbox (Gmail Takeout, Thunderbird, Apple Mail) and .eml files. Big files are fine; only headers are read.

How to get your mailbox file

  1. Gmail: go to takeout.google.com, click "Deselect all", tick only Mail, export, and unzip the .mbox file.
  2. Outlook / Hotmail / Yahoo / iCloud: add the account to Thunderbird, then right-click the Inbox → "Save as / Export" (ImportExportTools NG add-on) to .mbox.
